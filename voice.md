## Off limits

- **Topics I do not post about:** Rhinoplasty (Dr. Lee doesn't do it).
- **Clients, numbers or names I cannot say publicly:** Patient names or
  identifying details unless the patient has given written consent. Only post
  surgical footage that has documented consent.
- **Claims I am not allowed to make:** Never "guaranteed" or "risk-free".
  Show before-and-afters only with consent.
- **Gray-area treatments (exosomes, peptides):** We offer them; talk about
  them carefully so posts don't get flagged. Explain what the treatment is, how
  it's done and what patients tell us, using "may help", "designed to" and
  "supports". Never call them FDA-approved, never promise they cure or treat a
  disease, and give comparisons (e.g. vs PRP) as how we see it, not as proven
  fact.
