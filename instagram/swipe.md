## Read by hand (Nov 12, 2026)

**Sample:** 30 reels from 5 accounts, about 6 per account, read from the
cover text on Reels-tab screenshots, not the spoken first line. Dropped for
size: @oliverchangmd (572K) and @thedallasaesthetician (396K). Not yet
collected: @austinplasticsurgeon, @drlee90210, @nycgyno.

**Baselines:** median of each account's visible, non-pinned reels. Pinned
reels can be months old and have had longer to collect views, so their
multiples are inflated (Farzaneh's top three, Hamilton's tummy tuck).
Farzaneh's baseline comes from only 3 reels.

**Confidence:** low. Treat this as a direction to test, not a rule.

### Patterns in the top third (2x and up)

1. **"When…" meme caption over real OR footage** (3 reels: 463x, 38.8x,
   5.8x). A relatable work moment ("when your intern is trying to help")
   over unpolished footage from the OR. All three come from one account
   (@drbabakfarzaneh), so this is one creator's format, not a proven trend.
2. **Borrowed phrase or seasonal wordplay** (3 reels, 2 accounts: 50.5x,
   6.4x, 2.5x). A dating meme ("if he wants to text you, he would"), a
   month pun ("it's NO-vember"), and "POV: your patient is dropping the
   craziest lore". Counter-example: "pov: it's staff treatment day" at 0.9x.
3. **Visual transformation with plain words** (9.5x, 2.1x, 1.7x). Body
   results with short cover text or none. The 9.5x tummy tuck is pinned.
4. **Questions patients are embarrassed to ask** (2.6x, a two-person Q&A).

### What sits at or below 1x

- Covers that are just a product or treatment name: Alloclae, Xerf, CO2,
  "fat grafting vs Alloclae vs filler". All 0.4–1.1x.
- Logo cards, holiday family posts, "what do you make as a plastic surgeon?" (0.3x).

### Formulas not in hooks.json yet

- "When [relatable work moment]" meme caption over raw footage
- A borrowed pop-culture phrase applied to surgery
